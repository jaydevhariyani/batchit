About Batchit

Welcome to Batchit, the internet's most dynamic and secure platform for meeting new people. Born out of the need for a safer, faster, and more feature-rich environment after the closure of legacy platforms, Batchit has quickly become the ultimate Omegle alternative for millions of users worldwide.

Our Mission: To create a borderless world where anyone can instantly connect, practice languages, and talk to strangers online in a safe, moderated, and judgment-free zone.

Why We Built Batchit

For over a decade, anonymous chatting was dominated by platforms that failed to evolve. When looking for an OmeTV alternative or a place for random video chat with strangers, users were met with bots, poor moderation, and outdated interfaces. We launched Batchit in 2026 to change that. We integrated real-time matchmaking, face-to-face calling, online women's and men's live chatting, and robust moderation tools.

More Than Just Random Chat

Unlike traditional chatroulette alternatives, Batchit isn't just about fleeting moments. We introduced the ability to actually make friends. With our unique "Add Friend" system, if you have a great conversation, it doesn't have to end when the connection drops. You can filter by gender, country, and interests to ensure you're matching with people who share your vibe.

Safety First

We believe that free text chat and live video chatting should be fun, not creepy. Our 24/7 AI-driven moderation and community reporting tools ensure that Batchit remains a respectful platform. We give you complete control to report and block malicious users instantly.
