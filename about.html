About BatchIt

BatchIt is a free collection of online utility tools built for speed, simplicity and privacy.

What we offer

We provide practical everyday tools including:

  • PDF tools – Merge PDF, Resize PDF, Image to PDF
  • Image tools – Compress & Resize images
  • Finance calculators – SIP, EMI, GST, Percentage, Age
  • Text & developer tools – Word Counter, Password Generator, QR Code, JSON Formatter and more

Privacy first

Almost all processing happens directly in your browser. Your files (PDFs, images, text) are not uploaded to our servers. We designed BatchIt so you can work quickly without creating an account and without worrying about where your data goes.

Our mission

To give students, freelancers, small businesses and everyday users simple, reliable tools that just work — free of charge, without signup, and with respect for privacy.
