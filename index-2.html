Crop PDF Online Free

Crop all pages of your PDF at once. Set margins in points and download. 100% private — files stay in your browser.

Tip: 72 points ≈ 1 inch. Increase values to crop more from each edge.

How to Crop a PDF Online

  1. Click “Select PDF file” and choose your document.
  2. Set Top, Bottom, Left and Right crop margins (in points).
  3. Click Crop & Download PDF.
  4. Your cropped PDF downloads automatically.
