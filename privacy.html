Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BatchIt (batchit.site). We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our free online tools.

2. Files and Data You Upload

All tools on BatchIt (PDF merge, PDF resize, image compress, calculators, etc.) run entirely in your browser.

  • We do not upload your files to any server.
  • We do not store, view, or share your PDFs, images, or text.
  • When you close the page, the data stays only on your device.

3. Information We Collect

We may collect limited technical information such as:

  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and approximate location (via IP, for analytics)
  • How you interact with the site (anonymous usage statistics)

This helps us improve the website. We do not collect personal details like name, email, or phone number unless you voluntarily contact us.

4. Cookies and Advertising

BatchIt may use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Basic site functionality
  • Analytics (understanding traffic)
  • Advertising (Google AdSense)

Google AdSense may use cookies to show relevant ads. You can manage ad personalization through your Google account settings. We do not control third-party cookies directly.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as:

  • Google AdSense (advertising)
  • Google Analytics (if enabled)
  • CDN providers for libraries (e.g. Tailwind, pdf-lib)

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our tools are general-purpose and not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Security

Because file processing happens in your browser, your files never leave your device through our tools. We use HTTPS to protect the connection to our website.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at:

Email: contact@batchit.site

(Or use the contact details on our About page.)
