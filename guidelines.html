Community Guidelines

Welcome to Batchit! To keep our platform safe and fun for everyone across our 190+ supported countries, please follow these core rules:

1. Be Respectful

Do not harass, bully, or threaten other users. Hate speech, racism, and discriminatory language will not be tolerated and will result in a permanent ban.

2. No Illegal Content

Do not share, promote, or request any illegal content, including illicit images, pirated material, or links to malicious websites.

3. Protect Your Privacy

We are a 100% anonymous platform. For your own safety, do not share your personal information (real name, address, phone number, or social media handles) with strangers.

4. Report and Block

If you encounter someone breaking these rules, use the disconnect button immediately to end the chat. You can also use our reporting features to flag inappropriate behavior to our moderation team.
